About the game

Copy any of these as they are, or cut them to fit.

One line

Slice fruit. Catch the beat.

Short

Fruit fly at you on the beat of house music through fun neon worlds: cut them with swords, burst them with glowing orbs, or bat them away with glow sticks.

Long

Fruit streams toward you on the beat, riding glowing trails through fun neon worlds. Cut, punch and bat your way through every track and keep your combo alive.

  • Three ways to play: Slice ’Em with two swords, Break ’Em with a glowing orb in each fist, or Hit ’Em with glow sticks.
  • Four modes: Beat Run, an endless trip that steps up a difficulty every few minutes; One Song, for a rank on a single track; Fruit Storm (Slice ’Em and Break ’Em), 90 seconds of every kind of fruit flying in, in groups of 2 to 5; and Zen, with no fail and no bombs.
  • Easy, Normal, Hard and Expert, plus modifiers: No Fail, No Bombs, Big Fruit, Fast Fruit, Ghost Fruit and Small Fruit.
  • 18 house tracks that mix into each other on the beat.
  • Seven worlds to fly through: Neon City, the Kaleidoscope Tunnel, Fractal Wonderland, Cosmic Highway, Liquid Dream, Hyperdrive and a see-through rainbow worm tunnel, with fireworks over the open sky.
  • Mint fruit for your left hand, pink for your right, golden bonus fruit, and bombs to dodge in the storms.
  • Party mode: two to eight players take turns on one headset, over one to three rounds.
  • Passthrough: play in your own room.
  • Comfort options: Calm intensity, fewer flashes, one-handed play, and Blade Fit to set your weapons’ angle and length.

About MW Design

MW Design is an independent studio run by Mike Wickens. It started in 2016 with Wear OS arcade titles and custom smartwatch faces, and today makes games and apps for Apple platforms, Android and Meta Quest. Its other Quest games are XGalaga VR, Modern Domination - Future Warfare and Mini Circuit Racers.
