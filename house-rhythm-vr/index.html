How it plays

Fruit on the beat

Fruit streams toward you on the beat, riding glowing trails through trippy neon worlds. Cut, punch and bat your way through every track and keep your combo alive.

03

Three ways to play

Slice ’Em with two swords, Break ’Em with a glowing orb in each fist, or Hit ’Em with glow sticks.

L·R

Mint left, pink right

Mint fruit is for your left hand and pink fruit for your right. Golden fruit is a bonus, and the storms bring bombs to dodge.

04

Four modes

Beat Run is an endless trip that steps up a difficulty every few minutes. One Song is for a rank on a single track. Fruit Storm, in Slice ’Em and Break ’Em, is 90 seconds of every kind of fruit, in groups of 2 to 5. Zen has no fail and no bombs.

EXP

Four difficulties

Easy, Normal, Hard and Expert, plus six modifiers: No Fail, No Bombs, Big Fruit, Fast Fruit, Ghost Fruit and Small Fruit.

18

Eighteen house tracks

Every track mixes into the next on the beat.

07

Seven worlds

Fly through Neon City, the Kaleidoscope Tunnel, Fractal Wonderland, Cosmic Highway, Liquid Dream, Hyperdrive and a see-through rainbow worm tunnel, with fireworks over the open sky.

2–8

Party mode

Two to eight players take turns on one headset, over one to three rounds.

EASE

Your room, your comfort

Play in your own room with passthrough. Choose Calm intensity, fewer flashes or one-handed play, and use Blade Fit to set your weapons’ angle and length.

Privacy policy

Nothing collected

Last updated 21 November 2026. House Rhythm for Meta Quest is published by MW Design.

What the game collects. Nothing. House Rhythm has no accounts, no online play, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. MW Design receives no data about you or how you play.

Meta’s ownership check. When the game starts, it asks the Meta Platform SDK to confirm that the Meta account on your headset owns House Rhythm (Meta’s entitlement check). This is the game’s only network call. Meta runs the check under its own privacy policy. The game only learns whether the check passed, and nothing from it reaches MW Design.

What it stores on your headset. Your scores, ranks and play stats; your settings, such as volumes, weapon fit, comfort options and your last mode and difficulty; your favourite tracks; and the Party mode player list and results. These are kept in the app’s private storage, never leave the headset, and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Permissions. When you switch on passthrough, the headset shows your room behind the game. No camera images are recorded, stored or sent. No microphone, hand tracking or eye tracking is used.

Children. House Rhythm collects no data from anyone, children included.
