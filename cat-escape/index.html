On your wrist

An endless runner, designed for a round screen

Unity-powered gameplay optimised for Wear OS: fast loading, lightweight performance and controls tuned for smartwatch hardware. Tap any screen to enlarge it.

Gameplay

Classic endless running, one-handed

Designed for short, addictive sessions that fit a coffee break. Swipe and tap controls keep it smooth with one hand, and the speed keeps rising until you slip.

  • Increasing speed and difficulty on every run
  • Dynamic obstacles that keep each run different
  • Coin collection and high-score tracking to beat your personal best
Shop & collectables

Spend your coins on an edge

Every coin you pick up goes towards items and upgrades in the in-game shop.

  • Magnet — pulls in all nearby coins automatically
  • x2 Coins — doubles your coin rewards
  • Invincibility — run through danger unharmed
  • Extra Life — one more chance to keep running
Customisation

Make the escape yours

Pick your runner, pick the time of day, and pick a hat.

  • Characters — Trash Cat and Rubbish Raccoon
  • Themes — Day and Night
  • Accessories — unlockable hats
