Privacy policy

Nothing collected

Last updated 30 September 2026. Classic Arcade is published by MW Design.

What the app collects. Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no analytics, advertising or accounts, and it does not contact any server of ours.

What it stores on your device. Game progress and settings only: high scores, best times, favourites, saved games and your sound, music and haptics options. These stay in the app’s private storage and are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple’s Game Center, which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The app works fully without signing in.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
