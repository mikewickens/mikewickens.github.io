How it plays

Anyone can clear a screen

Clearing it well is the game. One bomb is always lit, and it is never the one nearest you.

💣

Twenty bombs, one lit

Take the lit one and the chain climbs. Take any other and it breaks. The route is the whole decision.

🦸

Jump, then fly

The jump is enormous. Press again in the air and you hover — hold it, tap it, steer it. Nothing is out of reach if you commit.

🔴

The i-Bomb

The last bomb is red, and it only appears once every black one is gone. It lands somewhere new every single run.

🗺️

Two tours, 24 stages

Twelve single-screen Classic stages, and twelve Arcade stages three screens wide with fifty bombs apiece.

⚡

Seven power-ups

Shield, Magnet, Time, Double score, Overcharge and a one-up — earned by playing well, not by waiting.

🎮

Play it your way

Keyboard, Xbox pad, PlayStation pad or touch controls, with safe-area layout on handhelds.
