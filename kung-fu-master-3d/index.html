What you get

A real fighting game, on every Apple screen

VS

Versus mode

One-on-one fights for two players on one screen, each with a controller, or against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard. Best of 1, 3 or 5 rounds.

10F

A ten-floor story

From the temple corridor through the night market, the Great Wall, the Lotus Pavilion and the Dragon’s Den, to Master Ryuga in his palace.

MOCAP

Motion-captured martial arts

Fighters react to hits high, middle and low, stagger, fall hard and get back up. Back and kick for the new Power Kick.

SP

A special for everyone

Fill the gold meter and let loose: Dragon Palm, Phoenix Dive, Earthquake, Crane Kick and more. Some stun, some freeze.

DASH

Fighting-game footwork

Dash in, run, backdash out of trouble, with a camera that closes in on the action and pulls back as fighters part.

PAD

Play your way

On-screen joypad on iPhone and iPad, game controllers everywhere, the Siri Remote on Apple TV and the keyboard on Mac.
