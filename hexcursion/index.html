Hexcursion: World TourSpin the hexagon. Match three. Travel the world.

A fast, colourful puzzle game built in 3D. Blocks fall towards a hexagon from every side: spin it to catch them, stack matching colours and clear three or more to score, across ten stunning locations from Bora Bora to neon Tokyo. Easy to learn, hard to stop.

Five ways to play

Journey through all ten locations, Time Attack for three minutes, Sprint to clear 100 blocks, Zen with no game over, and a Daily challenge that’s the same for everyone.

Privacy policy

Nothing collected

Last updated 12 November 2026. Hexcursion: World Tour is published by MWDESIGN.

What the app collects. Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no analytics, advertising or accounts, and it does not contact any server of ours.

What it stores on your device. Game progress and settings only: your best scores, the mode you last played and your sound and control settings. These stay in the app’s private storage and are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple’s Game Center, which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The app works fully without signing in.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
