Mini Circuit RacersArcade circuit racing, from the driver’s seat.

Strap into the cockpit and race three rivals round eight circuits. Hold the brake through a hairpin and the car swings round in a drift. Grab spinning power nuts for a turbo, a homing spare tyre or an oil slick for the car behind. Race the computer, or take up to four headsets online.

How it plays

Small cars, big racing

Pick up and race: the trigger is your throttle and the stick steers. The rest you learn by the first hairpin.

DRIFT

Drift on the brake

Hold the brake as you turn, and the car swings round the bend at an angle and keeps its speed. Let go to straighten up. Barriers slide you along, not stop you dead.

08

Eight circuits

Lakeside Park, Forest Speedway, Desert Sprint, Neon Strip and Hillside Ring on tarmac, plus Mudlark Rallycross, Ridgeback Hills and Eightwood Crossover on dirt, with jumps.

04

Four cars

The nimble SPRINT, the grippy GT3, the off-road BUGGY and the fast HYPERCAR, each in your team colours.

NUTS

Pickups

Drive through the spinning power nuts for a TURBO, a homing SPARE TYRE to throw at the car ahead, or an OIL slick to drop behind you.

CUP

Quick races or a championship

Jump straight into a race, or run a full championship where qualifying sets the grid. Computer drivers on EASY, NORMAL or HARD.

04

Race online

Host a race or join one from the list: up to four headsets over the internet, with computer drivers filling the rest of the grid.

VIEW

Your view, your way

Switch between the cockpit, a chase view and a top-down view mid-race, raise the seat, and move the HUD panels to the corners if you prefer a clear view.

EASE

Made for comfort

The horizon stays level, there’s no camera shake, and a hit never spins your view. Play seated.

Privacy policy

Nothing collected by MW Design

Last updated 18 November 2026. Mini Circuit Racers for Meta Quest is published by MW Design.

What MW Design collects. Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking by MW Design and no MW Design accounts.

Racing on your own. Quick races and championships against the computer use no network connection.

Online races. These use Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay), run by Unity Technologies. The game signs in anonymously, and Unity creates a random player ID that is stored on your headset. No email address or Meta account is used. Your headset’s device name (for example “QUEST 3”) is shown to the other players as your name. A hosted race’s listing holds the host’s device name, the circuit and whether the race has started, and it is deleted when the host leaves. Car positions pass through Unity’s relay servers to the other players, and, like any internet service, Unity processes your IP address to do this. Unity’s handling of that data is covered by Unity’s privacy policy.

What it stores on your headset. Your settings: your car and team colours, circuit, laps, difficulty, view, seat height, HUD layout, steer help and volume. These are kept in the app’s private storage and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Permissions. Internet access is used for online races only. No cameras, microphone, hand tracking or eye tracking are used.

Children. No data is knowingly collected from children, and MW Design collects none from anyone.
