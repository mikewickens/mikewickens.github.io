TouchReliefSomewhere to put your hands.

An anti-stress app for when your head is busy. Press into slime and it dents, drag ink and it swirls, let go and it settles. Every texture is simulated live on your own device, so there is not one video or looping animation in the app.

The tour

A toybox and a calm-down kit

Ten screens from the iPhone app. Swipe or use the arrows, and tap any screen to enlarge it.

Textures

Nothing is pre-rendered

Each texture is a live simulation running on your device’s GPU with Metal. That is why it responds to exactly where you press, and why it never repeats itself.

  • Slimes — a height field with viscous relaxation, eleven materials, glitter and multi-touch
  • Fluids — ink, smoke, water, mercury and oil, solved as a real velocity field
  • Particles — thirteen behaviours: strands, sparks, galaxy, warp, sand, snow and more
  • Orbs — plasma ball, storms, lava lamp, crystal ball and a black hole
  • Kaleidoscope — mirror-fold any engine, plus Julia, Mandelbrot and mandala fractals
  • DIY slime — build your own: texture, three colours, sound, feel and vibration
Gadgets

Nineteen 3D toys with real physics

Hand-modelled objects rendered live with physically based materials, so they react to your touch instead of playing back a clip. Each has its own sound and Core Haptics signature.

  • A fidget spinner with bearing drag and detents, in five designs
  • Newton’s Cradle with chrome spheres and elastic transfer between the balls
  • An ice cube that cracks along its seams, bursts, then refreezes
  • A tuned kalimba, chime bars, a neon harp and a pin-art press
  • Gyroscopic rings, magnetic balls, a lava lamp, a switch bank and an abacus that counts
Breathe & mindfulness

Guided by a real voice

Five breathing patterns, including 4-7-8 and box breathing, with a flower that opens and closes in time and gentle haptics on every phase. Every meditation, sleepcast and practice is narrated by a recorded voice rather than a speech synthesiser.

  • Seven meditations and seven mindfulness practices, including 5-4-3-2-1 grounding and a one-minute pause
  • A silent timer with singing-bowl bells
  • Affirmation sessions picked for today’s focus, over particles that change shape with each line
Sleep

Wind down, then drift off

Four narrated sleepcasts, a mixer for layering rain, ocean, fire and birds, and a Bedside mode that turns the phone into a slow lava lamp with a clock and a dimmer.

  • 24 sounds: recorded beds crossfaded between takes so the loop never lands on the beat, plus white, pink and brown noise synthesised live
  • Twelve ready-made Relax Mixes, or save your own
  • A sleep timer that fades everything out, from 15 to 90 minutes
Music visualiser

Your music, moving every texture

Play from your library, a file, or the microphone and every texture reacts. Bass drives slime dents and fluid splashes, highs pulse the glow, and timed switches land on the beat.

  • Live FFT analysis with a 32-bar spectrum and tempo detection
  • The microphone picks up music from any app or speaker. Audio is analysed on device and never stored.
  • Ships with Volcano Run, an original track from XGalaga HD
Point Clouds

30,000 particles, ten shapes

A geodesic sphere, solar jets, a nebula, a heart, Saturn and a lotus. Drag to spin, hold to attract, tap to burst. Affirmation sessions morph the cloud with every spoken line.

Check in

Five minutes, built from how you feel

A 20-second daily check-in covers mood, stress, energy, sleep and focus, and gives you a 0–100 Mental Index with a trend chart. Save it and TouchRelief proposes a routine that fits: two minutes of texture, two of breathing, one of affirmations.

Apple Watch

Calm on your wrist, and a remote for your phone

The watch app works on its own, and it can also drive the iPhone app from across the room.

  • Calm Touch — 220 glowing particles orbit your finger, with Taptic clicks
  • Breathe — the same five patterns, with directional haptics on each phase
  • Sounds — twelve ambient sounds played on the watch itself
  • Check-in, affirmations and a mindful timer with haptic bells
  • iPhone Remote — change textures, start a routine, set the sleep timer, turn on Bedside mode
iPad

Room to spread out

Under the hood

Simulated, not played back

Written natively in Swift with SwiftUI, Metal, RealityKit, AVAudioEngine and Core Haptics.

Slime

A height field with viscous relaxation. Press and it dents, drag and the surface smears with your finger, release and it settles under its own tension.

Fluids

A GPU stable-fluids solver with vorticity confinement, so a swirl keeps turning after your finger has gone.

Particles

Compute-shader particles that chase the touch with drag and turbulence rather than a scripted path.

Gadgets

RealityKit objects with physically based materials and a studio environment map generated on device.

Sound

Thirteen touch-sound profiles driven by finger speed and pressure. Noise beds are synthesised live; rain, birds and fire are recordings crossfaded between four takes.

Haptics

A continuous Core Haptics texture player plus transients, in four strength levels.
