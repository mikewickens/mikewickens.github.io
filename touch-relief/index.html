Nothing is pre-rendered

Every texture is simulated, not played back

There is no video in the app and no looping animation. Each texture is a live simulation running on your device’s GPU, which is why it responds to exactly where you press and never repeats itself.

01

Slime

A height field with viscous relaxation. Press and it dents, drag and the surface smears along with your finger, release and it settles back under its own tension.

02

Fluids

Ink, smoke and water solved as a velocity field, so a swirl keeps turning after your finger has gone.

03

Particles

Thousands of lights that chase the touch, with drag and turbulence rather than a scripted path.

04

Gadgets

Nineteen hand-modelled 3D toys with real physics — a spinner with bearing drag and detents, gyroscopic rings you flick, magnetic balls that snap together.

05

Sound

The touch sounds and the noise beds are synthesised as you play, so nothing loops. Rain, birds and fire are recordings, crossfaded between several takes so the repeat never lands on the beat.

06

Haptics

Every texture and toy has its own Core Haptics signature, tuned to the thing it is meant to feel like.

Who it is for

Built for restless hands and busy heads

Anxiety and panic spikes, ADHD restlessness, sensory overload and stimming, nail-biting, and the sort of rumination that keeps you awake. A section inside the app maps each of those to the exercise that helps, so you are never left staring at a menu.

·

Breathe

Five patterns including 4-7-8 and box breathing, with a flower that opens and closes in time, gentle haptics and a spoken guide.

·

Sleep

Narrated sleepcasts and meditations, a mixer for layering rain, ocean, fire and birds, and a Bedside mode with a slow lava lamp and a timer that fades everything out.

·

Your music

Play from your library, a file or the microphone and every texture moves to the beat.

·

Low Sensory mode

Softens motion, brightness, sound and haptics across the whole app, and hides the flashing textures.

TouchRelief is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and is not intended to diagnose or treat any condition.
