How it plays

A war room, a table, a world

The same turn-based conquest as Modern Domination on Android, laid out on a 3D war table you can lean over.

3D

A 3D war table

Mountains, deserts, forests and rivers rise from the map. Tanks, drones, cruise missiles and infantry soldiers stand on the board.

WALL

The battle feed

A video wall in the bunker shows clips of the fighting as you attack.

06

Six maps

World, Europe, Africa, Asia, Americas and the British Isles. Pick a theatre and a starting rule, one of eight.

CPU

Three CPU opponents

Set each CPU to Easy, Medium or Hard. Or pass and play with friends on one headset.

04

Up to four headsets

Play over local Wi-Fi or the internet. Pick a game from the list, choose your colour in the lobby, and the host presses Start. CPUs play every colour nobody took, and a player who drops out is replaced by the CPU.

Mixed reality lets you place the table in your own room with passthrough.

Privacy policy

Nothing collected by MW Design

Last updated 14 November 2026. Modern Domination: Future Warfare for Meta Quest is published by MW Design.

What MW Design collects. Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking by MW Design and no MW Design accounts.

On your own, or pass and play. No network connection is used.

Local Wi-Fi play. Headsets on the same Wi-Fi connect directly to each other. Your headset’s device name (for example “QUEST 3”) is shown to the other players. Nothing leaves your network.

Internet play. This uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay), run by Unity Technologies. The game signs in anonymously, and Unity creates a random player ID that is stored on your headset. No name, email address or Meta account is used. A hosted game’s listing holds the host headset’s device name, the map, the number of players and when the game starts, and it is deleted when the host leaves. Game moves pass through Unity’s relay servers to the other players, and, like any internet service, Unity processes your IP address to do this. Unity’s handling of that data is covered by Unity’s privacy policy.

What it stores on your headset. Your settings, your saved match and the position of the table in your room. These are kept in the app’s private storage and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Permissions. Internet access is used for multiplayer only. The passthrough cameras are used only to show your room in mixed reality; nothing is recorded or sent. Hand tracking is used for input. Spatial anchors remember where the table stands in your room, and are kept on the headset.

Children. The game is suitable for ages 12 and up. No data is knowingly collected from children, and MW Design collects none from anyone.
