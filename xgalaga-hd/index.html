Every pixel generated

There is not one bitmap in this game

Not a single PNG ships in the app bundle. Every ship, bolt, power-up and explosion is rendered on your device at first launch by a purpose-built offline renderer, then cached to disk. It looks pre-rendered because it is — just on your hardware, at your display’s resolution.

01

Geometry

Ships are parametric vector hulls — six alien archetypes (dart, saucer, crystal, beetle, raptor, hive) crossed with wing styles, armour, claws and crests.

02

Relief

Each part is rasterised to an anti-aliased mask; a chamfer distance transform drives a blended bevel and dome height field, whose gradient gives per-pixel surface normals.

03

Material

Procedural fBm noise and brushed-metal streaks perturb the height field, so the detail is shading rather than decals.

04

Lighting

A fixed three-point rig — key, cool fill, back rim — with metallic Blinn-Phong, plus an analytic environment probe for reflections. That last term is what makes metal read as metal.

05

Occlusion

Distance-field ambient occlusion, plus contact shadows cast between parts, layer to layer.

06

Emission

Cores and running lights bloomed with a three-radius filter, then ACES tone-mapped to sRGB.

How it plays

Faithful where it counts

The wave timings, entry flight paths and formation behaviour are reproduced exactly from the 1998 original. What’s new is everything you see and hear.

🛸

Seventeen alien classes

Six hull families across four threat tiers — from teal scout drones through amber raptors and violet shardlords to the gold-crested Dreadnought and Leviathan.

🎯

Fifteen hand-authored waves

All fifteen levels of the original, looping with rising aggression. Nothing is generated at random — the routes are the routes.

⚡

Eleven power-ups

Twin, tri and spread cannons. Autoloader, ammo cells, thrusters, deflector shields, smart bombs, extra ships and bounties.

⏱️

Locked 60 Hz

A fixed-step simulation that stays smooth at 120 Hz on ProMotion, so the timing is identical on every device.

💥

Built to feel right

Camera shake, hit-stop, and slow motion when you go down. Engine trails, ion wakes, shockwaves and tumbling hull debris.

🚀

A ship that grows

Your fighter visibly upgrades as the loadout builds — new barrels, new plating, new light.

🔒
