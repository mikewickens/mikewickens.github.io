How it plays

Faithful where it counts

The wave timings, entry flight paths and formation behaviour are reproduced exactly from the 1998 original. What’s new is everything you see and hear.

All fifteen levels of the original, looping with rising aggression. Nothing is generated at random: the routes are the routes.

Every pixel generated

There is not one bitmap in this game

Not a single PNG ships in the app bundle. Every ship, bolt, power-up and explosion is rendered on your device at first launch by a purpose-built offline renderer, then cached to disk. It looks pre-rendered because it is, just on your hardware and at your display’s resolution.

Geometry

Ships are parametric vector hulls: six alien archetypes (dart, saucer, crystal, beetle, raptor, hive) crossed with wing styles, armour, claws and crests.

Relief

Each part is rasterised to an anti-aliased mask. A chamfer distance transform drives a blended bevel and dome height field, whose gradient gives per-pixel surface normals.

Material

Procedural fBm noise and brushed-metal streaks perturb the height field, so the detail is shading rather than decals.

Lighting

A fixed three-point rig of key, cool fill and back rim, with metallic Blinn-Phong plus an analytic environment probe for reflections. That last term is what makes metal read as metal.

Occlusion

Distance-field ambient occlusion, plus contact shadows cast between parts, layer to layer.

Emission

Cores and running lights bloomed with a three-radius filter, then ACES tone-mapped to sRGB.
