Kung FuTemple of the Red Dragon. Eight floors. One fighter. No way back down.

A modern take on the arcade classic. Fight your way up an eight-floor pagoda through temples, night markets, the Great Wall, a serpent hall, bamboo forests, waterfalls and snow, to the palace where the master waits. Hand-painted 16-bit pixel art, living animated backdrops and an original soundtrack.

How it plays

Classic climb, modern feel

The one-lane, floor-by-floor climb of the 1984 original, rebuilt with a full move set, readable enemy tells and a proper sense of arrival at every floor.

9

A full move set

Punches, kicks, crouching punches and sweeps, a jump punch that strikes down, a flying kick, a guard and a roll.

8F

Eight painted floors

Temple, night market, the Great Wall, a serpent hall, bamboo forest, waterfall bridge, snow monastery and the sunset palace.

LIVE

Backdrops that breathe

Petals drift, steam rises from the market stalls, waterfalls pour and snow falls behind the fight.

BOSS

Bosses with tells

Brutes, throwers and bosses telegraph their attacks. Block, jump the sweeps or roll clear of the heavy hits.

HAZ

Hazards, like the original

Snakes, falling pots and a fire-breathing dragon on the serpent floor, just as the arcade had.

CLEAR

Every floor earned

Claim the dragon medallion, count up your bonuses and watch Kai climb the pagoda map to the next floor.

SFX

Sound that hits

Punchy recorded impacts, a retro fight announcer and a different track on every floor.

PAD

Play your way

Touch, keyboard or controller, with Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro pads supported.

Privacy policy

Nothing collected

Last updated 28 September 2026. Kung Fu: Temple of the Red Dragon is published by MW Design.

What the app collects. Nothing. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts or network features.

What it stores on your device. Game progress and settings only: your high score and your sound, screen shake and display options. These stay in the app’s private storage, never leave your device, and are removed when you delete the app.

Children. The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
