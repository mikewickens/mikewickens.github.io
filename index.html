The studio

One studio. Every screen.

MW Design is an independent studio run by Mike Wickens. It started in 2016 with Wear OS arcade titles and custom smartwatch faces, and today ships native games and apps across the Apple ecosystem, Android and Meta Quest.

Every title is built from the engine up for the hardware it runs on, from the Digital Crown on a watch to a 4K television or a headset.

01

Native on every platform

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch from one purchase where we can, with touch, keyboard, controller, Siri Remote and Digital Crown all supported.

02

Technology you can see

XGalaga HD renders every sprite on device. TouchRelief simulates every texture live. Chroma Vector runs on a Metal renderer written from scratch.

03

Made in-house

Design, code, level building and original soundtracks, from i-Bomb’s thirteen tracks to XGalaga HD’s score.

04

Respect for players

Our paid Apple titles are buy once: no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking.
