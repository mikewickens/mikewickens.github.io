The studio

One studio. Every screen.

MW Design is an independent studio run by Mike Wickens. It started in 2016 with Wear OS arcade titles and custom smartwatch faces, and today ships native games and apps across the Apple ecosystem, Android and Meta Quest.

Every title is built from the engine up for the hardware it runs on, from the Digital Crown on a watch to a 4K television or a headset.
